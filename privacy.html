Privacy Policy

What stays in your Kingdom belongs to you.

Effective date: October 18, 2026 · Applies to Kingdom Wayfinder version 1.0

Overview

Kingdom Wayfinder is an iPhone day-planning app that works with Apple Calendar. This policy explains how the app uses information to provide its scheduling features.

Calendar Access

With your permission, Kingdom accesses Apple Calendar information to display existing commitments, identify open time, and help you build and apply a daily schedule. Calendar access is used to provide the features you choose to use. You can manage or revoke the app's calendar permissions through iPhone Settings.

Storage and Accounts

Kingdom Wayfinder version 1.0 stores its app information locally on your iPhone and works with Apple Calendar. Kingdom does not require you to create an account and does not provide its own cloud-sync service.

Calendar events that you choose to save through Apple Calendar may be synchronized by your calendar provider under that provider's settings and privacy practices. Kingdom does not control those services.

Analytics, Advertising, and Sharing

Kingdom Wayfinder version 1.0 does not integrate third-party analytics or advertising services. The app developer does not operate a server to collect or sell your calendar information.

Support Emails

If you contact support, the information you choose to include in your email will be used to respond to your inquiry. Email is handled through the support mailbox's email provider, separate from the Kingdom app.

Your Choices

You can change calendar permissions in iPhone Settings. You can manage calendar events using Apple Calendar. Removing the app may remove locally stored app information, subject to iOS behavior and device backups.

Changes to This Policy

If Kingdom's features or data practices change, this policy will be updated to reflect those changes.

Contact
