Getting Started

1. Grant calendar access. Kingdom uses Apple Calendar access to show your scheduled commitments and work with your daily plan. You can review calendar permissions in your iPhone Settings.

2. Build your day. Review the commitments and open windows shown in Kingdom. Add your Kingdom Blocks to available times.

3. Arrange your plan. Adjust block start times and durations, and review any scheduling conflicts.

4. Apply when ready. Review the day before choosing to apply Kingdom Blocks to Apple Calendar.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does Kingdom replace Apple Calendar?
No. Kingdom works alongside Apple Calendar to help you plan around existing commitments.

Do I need an account?
No. Kingdom Wayfinder V1 does not require an account.

Where is my information stored?
Kingdom V1 uses on-device storage and Apple Calendar. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

Something looks wrong with my schedule. What should I do?
Review your calendar access and your selected day. If the problem continues, email support with a description of the issue.
